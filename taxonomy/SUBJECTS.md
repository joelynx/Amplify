# Subjects and boundary rules

The question bank (about 21,700 questions: textbook exercises, exam problems, olympiad and puzzle
collections) is being sorted into 22 subjects. Each subject holds a tree Topic > Branch > Subtopic.

Classify by the MAIN IDEA needed to solve the question, not by its story or by its source.

| # | Subject | Scope |

Boundary rules
- A question asking for a probability or an expectation is Probability, even when it is solved by counting. Pure counting is Combinatorics.
- Inequalities proved with algebraic tools are Algebra; with derivatives, integrals or limits, Calculus.
- Physics: anything that reasonably fits Mechanics goes there (including elasticity, oscillations, mechanical waves); liquids and gases in motion or at rest go to Fluid Mechanics; heat, gas laws and phase changes to Thermodynamics; electricity, magnetism, circuits, optics and relativity to Electromagnetism and Special Relativity; atoms, nuclei and quanta to Quantum Mechanics.
- Every question from R.K. Rajput's or P.K. Nag's Power Plant Engineering goes to Conventional Energy Technologies.
- Srinath's Advanced Mechanics of Solids goes to Mechanics.
- Puzzle, olympiad and interview collections are split by content across subjects.
- If nothing fits, write `Other: <short suggested subject>` (for example `Other: Algorithms`).
