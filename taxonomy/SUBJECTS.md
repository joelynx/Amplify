# Subjects and boundary rules

The question bank (about 21,700 questions: textbook exercises, exam problems, olympiad and puzzle
collections) is being sorted into 26 subjects (23-26 added 2026-11-23). Each subject holds a tree Topic > Branch > Subtopic.

Classify by the MAIN IDEA needed to solve the question, not by its story or by its source.

Boundary rules
- A question asking for a probability or an expectation is Probability, even when it is solved by counting. Pure counting is Combinatorics.
- Inequalities proved with algebraic tools are Algebra; with derivatives, integrals or limits, Calculus.
- Physics: anything that reasonably fits Mechanics goes there (including elasticity, oscillations, mechanical waves); liquids and gases in motion or at rest go to Fluid Mechanics; heat, gas laws and phase changes to Thermodynamics; electricity, magnetism, circuits, optics and relativity to Electromagnetism and Special Relativity; atoms, nuclei and quanta to Quantum Mechanics.
- Every question from R.K. Rajput's or P.K. Nag's Power Plant Engineering goes to Conventional Energy Technologies.
- Srinath's Advanced Mechanics of Solids goes to Mechanics.
- Puzzle, olympiad and interview collections are split by content across subjects.
- Chemistry vs Thermodynamics: gas laws and kinetic theory stay in Thermodynamics; a question about a chemical reaction's energetics or equilibrium is Chemistry.
- Chemistry vs Quantum Mechanics: atomic physics and radioactive decay stay in Quantum Mechanics; molecular structure, bonding and molecular spectroscopy are Chemistry.
- Chemistry vs Material Science: crystal structure, defects and phase diagrams of materials stay in Material Science; the solid state as general chemistry (packing, unit-cell density) follows the source's course.
- Chemical Engineering vs Heat Transfer, Fluid Mechanics and Thermodynamics: heat exchangers and process heat-transfer equipment stay in Heat Transfer; pipe flow stays in Fluid Mechanics; mass transfer and separations are Chemical Engineering; phase and reaction equilibria of mixtures are Chemical Engineering, pure-substance thermodynamics stays in Thermodynamics.
- Chemical Engineering vs Signals, Systems and Control: process-control questions about plants and loops are Chemical Engineering; control theory posed on abstract transfer functions or servo systems is Signals, Systems and Control.
- Circuits and Electronics vs Electromagnetism and Special Relativity: physics-course circuits (DC networks, RC/RL/RLC transients, AC phasors) stay in Electromagnetism; network theorems with dependent sources, Laplace-domain analysis, two-ports, filters and all electronics are Circuits and Electronics.
- Trading and Market Making vs Probability: a question about quoting, sizing or edge in a market is Quantitative Finance; a pure expected-value or probability puzzle stays in Probability.
- If nothing fits, write `Other: <short suggested subject>` (for example `Other: Algorithms`).
